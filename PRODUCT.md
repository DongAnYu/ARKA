# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Learners who keep long-form notes in Obsidian and want to convert those notes into active-recall practice.
Most usage happens in focused desktop study sessions where users need fast import, clear status, and low-friction review.

## Product Purpose

Obsidian Active Recall turns local note vaults into study-ready practice content by chunking notes and generating question previews.
The product exists to reduce the gap between writing notes and retaining knowledge.
Success looks like users repeatedly loading vaults, generating previews, and quickly validating generated questions.

## Brand Personality

Focused, precise, and quietly confident.
The interface should feel like a dependable study instrument: clear hierarchy, calm pacing, and intentional interactions.

## Anti-references

- No noisy productivity-dashboard clutter with competing callouts.
- No playful consumer-style visuals that distract from study flow.
- No washed-out low-contrast dark UI where important actions disappear.

## Design Principles

- Prioritize flow clarity over visual novelty.
- Make progress and system status legible at a glance.
- Keep interaction patterns consistent and predictable.
- Reward core actions with subtle but meaningful motion feedback.
- Preserve cognitive focus by minimizing decorative noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for all primary and secondary text.
Provide visible focus states on interactive controls.
Respect reduced-motion preferences by minimizing non-essential animation.
Preserve keyboard accessibility for primary actions and view toggles.
