# Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process by which plants convert light energy into chemical energy. It mainly occurs in chloroplasts and uses carbon dioxide and water to produce glucose and oxygen.

## Main Stages

### 1. Light-dependent reactions

These reactions capture sunlight and produce:

- ATP
- NADPH
- Oxygen

### 2. Calvin cycle

The Calvin cycle uses ATP and NADPH to convert carbon dioxide into glucose.

## Overall Equation

**Carbon dioxide + water + light energy → glucose + oxygen**
